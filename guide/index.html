1. The short version

Gogo Guardians is an illustrated book with a riddle game built in, for ages 8 to 12. It is read online on any phone, tablet or computer. Nothing to install, and children never make an account.

Who is a partner? A person or group that signs up to share our link and earn: a parent, a teacher, a blogger, a homeschool group, a club or a local business. A school is not a partner. A school either keeps $3.00 from each family that buys on its page, or gives the book to a class.

Each sale pays one person, once. A family that gets a free seat from its school does not also buy, so a school's seat and a family purchase never pay twice.

2. Words we use

Unlock code

a personal code that starts with GOGO. It opens the whole book and game on up to 3 of a family's devices.A phone, a tablet and a computer is the usual mix. If a family replaces a device and the code says it is full, they write to admin@iddas.tech and we help.

School page

a page for one school on gogoguardians.com, for example gogoguardians.com/s/?school=your-school. It is free for the school. The school shares the link in its parent emails, newsletter or website. Nothing to install and nothing to embed.

Seat

one family's copy, paid for by the school. A class of 30 needs 30 seats.

Class code

one code the school sends to its families when it provides seats. Each family can use it once.The class code is set to the size of the class the school tells us. We raise it whenever the school asks, and we can switch it off at any time. The school pays only for the codes families use.

Partner

a person or group that shares Gogo Guardians with its own link and earns from it: a parent, teacher, blogger, homeschool group, club or local business. Joining is free. A school is not a partner.

Partner link

your own link from our store. Every sale through it is credited to you.

Bringing a school

asking for a school's page and getting a staff member at that school to confirm it. Then you earn on that school's sales.

Founders price

$14.95 for the whole adventure, one payment. If it ever changes, we give 30 days' notice by email first.

4. For schools

Two ways a school can use Gogo Guardians

A. Families buy on the school's own page. The school shares one link. Each family that wants it pays $7.49 instead of $14.95. The school pays nothing and keeps $3.00 from every family that buys, as fundraising. That is true whether the school found us by itself or a partner brought it.

B. The school provides seats. For a whole class or grade, the school gives the families one class code. Families pay nothing. The school pays $4.99 once for each family that uses the code, and that family keeps it for good. It is not a subscription: each month we send one invoice for the families who joined that month. Larger groups pay $2.99 a seat for every seat after the first 1,000 in total. A school can start with a free pilot class.

A school can use both: seats for one grade, the page for everyone else.

How a school gets its page

  1. Someone requests the page at gogoguardians.com/schools: the school's name, city, website, and the email of one staff member.
  2. If the staff email ends with the school's own website address, we email that staff member a link to confirm. Otherwise we check the school by hand, using the school's or district's own staff directory.
  3. Once a staff member confirms from the school email, the page goes live right away and we email the link. Schools we check by hand are ready within 24 hours on weekdays.

How seats work

  1. The school tells us which classes or grades at admin@iddas.tech. Nothing is paid up front.
  2. We send one class code.
  3. The school sends the class code to parents through its usual email or app. A paper slip home works for families without email.
  4. Each family enters the class code once and gets its own unlock code.
  5. Each month we send one invoice for the families who joined that month ($4.99 once per family, not a subscription). Invoices are due within 30 days. If an invoice is not paid, we switch the class code off until it is; families who already joined keep their copy. Each invoice comes with a usage statement: how many families used the class code, the date of each, and the last 4 characters of each family's unlock code so the school can check it. Nothing about children. Seats nobody uses cost nothing.

Fundraising

The school keeps $3.00 for every family that buys on its page. That is its fundraising, and it is the same whether the school found us by itself or a partner brought it. A school does not earn on seats it buys itself, because seats are already discounted. We pay it once a month to the school itself, by check or bank transfer in the school's name, never to a person. Before the first payment, we confirm the payment details with the school office.

5. For partners

Two ways to earn

1. Share your partner link. Join free at gogoguardians.com/partners. You earn 35% of every sale through your link: about $5.23 for every family at $14.95.

Your credit follows your shares. Make your link once on the partners page from your own phone. After that, every Share button on gogoguardians.com sends your link, so anyone who buys from what you shared is credited to you. Credit lasts 90 days from their visit.People often think it over first. If a family taps your link today and buys any time in the next 90 days, the sale is yours. If they click another partner's link later, the newest link gets the sale.

2. Bring a school. Request the school's page at gogoguardians.com/schools and add your partner key. When the school is confirmed, you earn on its sales:

  • $1.00 for every family that buys on the school's page (the school still keeps its own $3.00)
  • $2.00 a seat on the first 1,000 seats the school pays for
  • $1.00 a seat after the school or group passes 1,000 seats in total

Who gets credit for a school

  • Only one partner can earn on a school, never two. The school's own $3.00 is never touched: you earn from our share.
  • The first request the school confirms wins. Asking for a page does not lock anything.
  • The claim lasts 60 days from the school's confirmation. If nothing is bought through the school in 60 days, the school is open again. (This is about who brought a school. It is separate from the 90-day credit on your family link.)
  • Once a first purchase is made through the school, you keep the school for 12 months. Every new purchase restarts the 12 months.
  • Every step is recorded with its time in our Claims Ledger. If there is a dispute, the Claims Ledger decides it.

Rules for partners

  • Always say that you earn from your link.
  • No spam, no fake reviews, and no ads that use our name as a search keyword.
  • Only the prices and claims on our pages. No other discounts or promises.
  • Speak to parents, teachers and schools, never to children, and never collect a child's details.
  • No commission on purchases by you or your own household.

6. Examples with real numbers

A partner shares a link with friends

20 families buy at $14.95. The partner earns 20 × $5.23 = $104.60.

A partner brings a school, and families buy on its page

150 families buy at $7.49. The school keeps 150 × $3.00 = $450 as fundraising. The partner earns 150 × $1.00 = $150.

The same school provides seats for grade 4

120 families use them: 120 × $4.99 = $598.80, billed to the school that month. The partner earns 120 × $2.00 = $240. Those 120 families pay nothing.

A school network uses 2,000 seats over the year

The first 1,000 are $4.99 and the next 1,000 are $2.99: $4,990 + $2,990 = $7,980, billed month by month as seats are used. The partner earns 1,000 × $2.00 + 1,000 × $1.00 = $3,000.

A school signs up by itself

200 families buy on its page. The school earns 200 × $3.00 = $600 as fundraising.

7. Getting paid

  • We pay monthly by PayPal, Wise or Payoneer, your choice, in US dollars, for completed sales only.
  • Each sale is held 30 days so refunds and chargebacks can clear.Families can ask for a refund within 7 days, and a card dispute can take a few weeks. Holding each sale 30 days means we never have to take money back from you after a payout.
  • The minimum payout is $50. Smaller balances carry over to the next month.Small payments cost fees on both sides. Nothing is lost: the balance keeps growing until it passes $50, then it is paid.
  • No commission on refunded, canceled or charged-back sales.
  • US partners give us a W-9 before their first payout. You handle your own taxes.

8. Children's privacy

Gogo Guardians is bought by parents, guardians and schools. Children never make an account, and we collect nothing from children: no names, no emails, no student IDs. A child plays with a Guardian name that stays on the device. Read more at Privacy for families.

9. Questions

Tap a scroll to open it.

Does the school have to pay anything for its page?

No. The page is free. Families pay $7.49 each if they want it.

Can a school sell seat codes to parents?

No. Seat codes are given to the school's own families for free.

Can a partner and the school both earn on the same school?

Yes. The school always keeps $3.00 for every family that buys on its page. If a partner brought the school, the partner also earns $1.00 per family, paid from our share, so the school loses nothing.

Example: Maria, a mom who is a partner, tells her children's school, Lincoln Elementary, about Gogo Guardians, and Lincoln asks for its page through Maria's link. A family buys on Lincoln's page for $7.49. Lincoln keeps $3.00 as fundraising. Maria earns $1.00 from our share. Lincoln loses nothing because Maria brought it.

What if a family buys and then wants a refund?

They email admin@iddas.tech within 7 days and get a full refund, no questions asked. Their code stops working, and no commission is paid on that sale.

Will the price change?

There is no price change planned. If prices or partner earnings ever change, we give 30 days' notice by email first.

Does a school have to pay up front for seats?

No. The school pays $4.99 once for each family that uses the code, and each month we send one invoice for the families who joined that month. A free pilot class is available to start.

Is there a lower price than $2.99?

No. $2.99 a seat, for every seat after the first 1,000 a school or group uses in total, is our lowest price.

Where are the full terms?

In the Partner Terms. If this page and the Partner Terms ever differ, the Partner Terms decide.
