1. What you are sharing

Gogo Guardians is an illustrated read-and-solve adventure. A magic phone named Gogo takes Alibaba and the Forty Thieves across the universe. Kids read a chapter, then solve riddles to unlock the next world. 99 full-color pictures, and the first 3 chapters are free at gogoguardians.com.

Who buys: parents, grandparents and teachers of kids 8 to 12.

2. What you earn

35% of every book sold through your own link, about $5.23 on a $14.95 sale. When the book is on sale, you earn 35% of the sale price.

Paid monthly by PayPal, Wise or Payoneer. Full details: How it works.

3. Sign up in 2 minutes

  1. Read the partners page.
  2. Tap Join free as a partner. You create a partner account on Payhip, the store that runs our checkout (name, email, password).
  3. Already bought the book or have a Payhip account? Sign out of Payhip first, then join with a different email, like yourname+gogo@gmail.com. If Payhip asks you to name a store, it did not work: sign out and try again.
  4. You're approved, usually within a day.
  5. Log in at payhip.com/login, open your partner dashboard and copy your own Gogo Guardians link.

4. What to share

  • Your own book link, from your Payhip partner dashboard.
  • Your own trailer page: on the partners page, paste your Payhip link to get it.
  • The promoter kit: trailer, cover and ready-to-copy posts at gogoguardians.com/kit.
  • Never the plain website. Only your own links are credited to you.

  • Email only people who know you or asked. No cold email blasts with pictures and links.
