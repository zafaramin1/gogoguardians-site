Partner Terms

Plain words, fair to everyone. In effect from November 19, 2026.

Want it explained with examples first? See How it works.

These terms are between you and IDDAS Technologies LLC ("we"), which publishes Gogo Guardians. By joining as a partner, or by requesting a school page, you agree to them.

1. Prices and what you earn

  • Regular partner link: $14.95 Founders price until December 31, 2026, then $19.95. You earn 35% of the price paid (about $5.23 now, about $6.98 after December 31) on each completed sale.
  • Families on an approved school's page: $7.49. The person who brought the school earns $3.00 per sale.
  • School seats: $4.99 a seat, paid once per family that uses the class code, invoiced monthly. It is not a subscription. The person who brought the school earns $2.00 a seat on paid seats.
  • Larger groups: once a school, district or network passes 1,000 seats used in total, every seat after that is $2.99, and the person who brought it earns $1.00 a seat. That is our lowest price. Free pilot classes earn nothing.
  • One person earns per school, never two, and the school share is never split. If a partner brought the school, the partner earns it. If the school signed up by itself, the school earns the family share ($3.00 per family) as fundraising. A school does not earn on seats it buys itself; those are already discounted.
  • A family who arrives through your link is credited to you for 90 days. If they click another partner's link later, the newest link gets the sale.
  • One sale, one commission. There are no levels: nobody earns on another partner's sales.
  • No commission on purchases by you or your own household, or on orders we judge to be fake or abusive. We may hold or reverse those earnings.

2. How a school is confirmed

  • A request is not a claim. Asking for a school page locks nothing.
  • We confirm one real staff member at the school. If the staff member's email ends with the school's own website address, we email them a link to confirm. Otherwise we verify the school by hand, using the school's or district's own staff directory.
  • No school page goes live until the school is confirmed and we approve it.

3. Who gets credit for a school

  • The first request that the school confirms wins, by the time of the confirmation.
  • That claim lasts 60 days from confirmation. If nothing is bought through the school in 60 days (no family purchase on its page and no seat order), the claim ends and the school is open again.
  • Once a first purchase is made through the school (a family on its page or a seat order), the partner keeps the school for 12 months. Every new purchase through the school restarts the 12 months.
  • A school that signs up by itself while another partner's claim is active stays with that partner until the claim ends.
  • Our Claims Ledger records every step with its time: the request, the school's confirmation, our approval and the first purchase. No entry is ever changed. If there is a dispute, the Claims Ledger decides it.

4. School seats

  • A seat is one family's copy, paid by the school. A school that provides seats gets one class code. It works once per family, and the school pays once for each family that uses it, on one invoice a month.
  • The school gives the class code to its own families for free. Seat codes may not be resold.

5. Getting paid

  • We pay monthly by PayPal, Wise or Payoneer (your choice), for completed sales only, after a 30-day hold, so refunds and chargebacks can clear.
  • The minimum payout is $50. Smaller balances carry over to the next month.
  • No commission is paid on refunded, canceled or charged-back sales. If one is reversed after you were paid, we take it off your next payout.
  • Payments are in US dollars. The payment service's own fees, if any, come off the payout. You need a working PayPal, Wise or Payoneer account; unpaid balances wait until you give us one.
  • You handle your own taxes. US partners give us a W-9 before their first payout.

6. How you promote

  • Always say that you earn from your link.
  • No spam: no bulk messages to people who did not ask, no fake reviews, no ads that use our name as a search keyword.
  • No promises we have not approved: no different prices, discounts or claims about the book.
  • Gogo Guardians is for families and schools. Speak to parents, teachers and schools, never to children, and never collect any child's details.

7. Who we are to each other

  • Partners are independent. You are not an employee or agent of IDDAS Technologies LLC, and you cannot sign anything or make promises for us.
  • We keep partner and school-staff contact details only to run this program, and we never sell them.

8. Ending and changes

  • We may end a partnership for breaking these terms. Earnings on completed sales before that are still paid, unless they came from breaking the terms.
  • We may change these terms with 30 days' notice by email. Continuing after that means you accept the change.

9. Limits and law

  • The book and our pages are provided as they are. Our total liability to a partner is limited to the commissions we owe that partner.
  • These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Wyoming, USA. IDDAS Technologies LLC is a Wyoming company.
