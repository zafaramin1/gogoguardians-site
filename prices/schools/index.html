Price list for schools

Two ways to bring Gogo Guardians to your families

Prices in US dollars. Valid from November 2026. We give 30 days' notice by email before any change to school prices.

1. Families buy it on your school's own page

Your school shares one link. Each family that wants it buys it.

$7.49per family, instead of the regular $14.95
Your school keeps $3.00 from every family that buys
  • The school pays nothing and has nothing to manage.
  • The $3.00 is the school's fundraising. It stays the school's even if a partner introduced your school to us: the partner is paid from our share.
  • Paid once a month to the school itself, by check or bank transfer in the school's name, never to a person.
  • The school price stays $7.49 when the regular price goes to $19.95 after December 31, 2026.

2. The school gives it to a class

The school sends one class code to its families. Families pay nothing.

$4.99once per family that uses the code
Not a subscription: each family keeps it for good
  • A seat counts only when a family enters the class code. Codes nobody enters cost nothing.
  • One invoice a month, for the families who joined that month, with a usage statement: how many families used the code and on which date. Nothing about children.
  • The class code is set to the size of your class. We raise it whenever you ask, and we can switch it off at any time.

3. Larger groups

Schools, districts and networks giving it to many classes.

$2.99per seat, for every seat after the first 1,000 in total
  • The first 1,000 seats are $4.99 each. Every seat after that is $2.99, our lowest price.

Free pilot class

Try it with one class first.

$0for one pilot class
  • Tell us which class at admin@iddas.tech. Nothing is paid up front.

What every family gets

  • The whole illustrated book: 21 chapters and 99 full-color pictures, read online on any phone, tablet or computer.
  • The riddle game: 85+ riddles from the story, short animations, a soundtrack and the Guardian certificate.
  • Their own unlock code, for up to 3 of the family's devices.
  • Children never make an account. No child names or emails, ever.
  • Families who pay can ask for a full refund within 7 days, no questions asked.
