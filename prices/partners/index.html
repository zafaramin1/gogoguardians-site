Price list for partners

What you earn for every family and every school you bring

A partner is a person or group that shares Gogo Guardians with its own link: a parent, teacher, blogger, homeschool group, club or local business. Joining is free. A school is not a partner. Amounts in US dollars, valid from November 2026. We give 30 days' notice by email before any change to partner earnings.

1. A family buys through your link

Anyone who buys the book after tapping your link or any Share button you sent.

$14.95Founders price, one payment
You earn 35%: about $5.23 per family

2. A family buys on the page of a school you brought

You introduced the school, and its families buy on the school's own page.

$8.99per family
You earn $1.00 per family. The school keeps its own $3.00.

3. A school you brought gives it to a class

The school pays once for each family that uses its class code.

$4.99per seat, paid once per family
You earn $2.00 per paid seat

4. Larger groups you brought

Schools, districts or networks past 1,000 seats in total.

$2.99per seat, for every seat after the first 1,000
You earn $1.00 per seat after the first 1,000

The rules, in short

  • Your credit lasts 60 days. If a family taps your link and buys any time in the next 60 days, the sale is yours. If they tap another partner's link later, the newest link gets the sale.
  • Each school is credited to one partner only. If two partners both bring the same school, the first one the school confirms gets the credit. The school always keeps its own $3.00 per family; you are paid from our share.
  • Each sale pays once. A family that gets a free seat from its school does not also buy, so nothing is paid twice.
  • Paid monthly by PayPal, Wise or Payoneer (we pick the one that works best in your country). Each sale is held 30 days so refunds can clear. The minimum payout is $25; smaller balances carry over and are paid every June 15 and December 15, however small. The payment service's fee comes off the payout.
  • No commission on refunded, canceled or charged-back sales. Families can get a full refund within 7 days, no questions asked.
