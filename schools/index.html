How students get in

  1. Families buy on your school's own page at gogoguardians.com. We send you the link to share in parent emails, the newsletter or on your website. Nothing to install. Or the school provides seats for a whole class or grade, paying monthly only for the seats used.
  2. For seats, the school emails one class code to parents through its usual parent email or app. It works once per family, and the school pays only for the codes used. A paper slip home is a backup for families without email.
  3. Parents enter the code at gogoguardians.com. No child names, emails or accounts. We collect nothing from children.

Questions schools ask

Do we pay anything up front?

No. The school page is free. If you provide seats for whole classes, you pay $4.99 once for each family that uses the code, and they keep it for good. It is not a subscription: one invoice a month for the families who joined that month. Invoices are due within 30 days. If an invoice is not paid, we switch the class code off until it is; families who already joined keep their copy. You can start with a free pilot class.

What exactly is a seat?

One family's copy, paid for by the school. A class of 30 needs up to 30 seats, and seats nobody uses cost nothing.

Do children make accounts?

No. Parents enter the code. No child names, emails or accounts, and we collect nothing from children.

How soon is our page ready?

Right away, in most cases. When a staff member confirms from the school's own email address, the page goes live the moment they click, and we email you the link to share.

If the school has no staff email we can check, we confirm it by hand within 24 hours on weekdays.

Can our families try it first?

Yes. The first 3 chapters and the first riddles are free, with no sign-up: read them here.

Does the school earn anything?

Yes, on families who buy. The school keeps $3.00 for every family that buys on its own page, as fundraising. That is the only amount, and it is the same for every school.

  • It is the same if a partner brought the school to us. The partner earns $1.00 per family from our share, so the school still keeps its full $3.00.
  • Example: Maria, a mom who is a partner, tells Lincoln Elementary about Gogo Guardians. A family buys on Lincoln's page for $8.99. Lincoln keeps $3.00. Maria earns $1.00 from our share.
  • Seats the school gives to a class earn nothing, because the price is already lowered for schools.The $3.00 fundraising is only on families who buy on the school's page. When the school pays for seats itself, there is no fundraising share, because the school is already getting the lowest price.
  • We pay the school once a month, by check or bank transfer in the school's name, never to a person. Before the first payment, we confirm the payment details with the school office.
